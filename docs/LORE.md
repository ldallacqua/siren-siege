# Siren Siege — Lore bible

The canon for every chat, bio, map and enemy. Keep new writing consistent with this file; add to it when you invent something. Tone: warm, flirty, a little melancholy under the teasing. Suggestive, never explicit (see AGENTS.md content policy). All heroines are adults.

## The world

**The Moonlit Isles** are a chain of islands under a very large, very close moon. Their people call the moon **the Lady**. The Lady is not quite a goddess, and she is not quite gone. She answers songs.

Beneath the islands lies **the Hollow Sea**, a drowned underworld where everything the world forgets sinks and goes hungry. Its hunger has a shape when it rises: **the Blight**.

**The Moonlit Shrine** stands on the last **Moongate**, a seal the first priestesses sang shut a thousand years ago. Every hundred years, on the night of the **Crimson Eclipse**, the Lady's light fails for one night, the seal thins and the Blight climbs up the shrine road in waves. This century the eclipse is close, and the seal is already cracking early.

## The Blight

The Blight is what's left when a memory is eaten. Its shapes are **layers** of stolen things wrapped around each other:

| In game         | In lore                                                                                           |
| --------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Mote            | A single lost memory: a name, a smell, a song. Drifts toward warmth.                              |
| Flicker         | Two memories tangled together; it has started to remember hunger.                                 |
| Glimmer         | A cluster bright enough to lure travelers off the road.                                           |
| Blaze           | A memory of panic, fast and burning.                                                              |
| Twin Wisp       | Two Blazes circling each other: a pair of people who forgot each other.                           |
| Iron Husk       | Grief that has hardened into armor. Ordinary weapons bounce off it; silver, fire and song do not. |
| Blight Colossus | A fragment of **the Hollow King**, the Hollow Sea's oldest hunger, testing the seal.              |

**Popping a layer frees what was inside.** That's the little light that floats up when an enemy pops: a memory going home. The heroines know it, and it's why they fight gently even when they fight hard.

## Sirens and the Commander

**Sirens** are women born from myth: vampires, snow spirits, oni, moon-touched priestesses. A Siren's power is a song, and a song needs someone to listen. Alone, a Siren slowly fades into legend. **Bound to a mortal who truly listens, she grows stronger.** That bond is _Bond_ (the in-game meter). It's also why Bond gives them a little extra power in battle.

Mortals who can hold a Siren's bond without being burned, frozen or drained are called **Anchors**. They are extremely rare. **The Commander (the player)** is one. Selene found them in a prophecy, "a heart the Lady can't see through", and sent for them when the old shrine keeper, **Keeper Haruo**, walked down into the Hollow Sea ten years ago to buy the seal more time and never came back.

The Commander's past is deliberately left blank so the player can fill it in. Never give them a gender, a face or a backstory the player didn't choose.

## The heroines

### Scarlet Vane — the Crimson Gunslinger

- **Born** four centuries ago as the youngest daughter of House Vane, in the **Crimson Keep** far to the west. She was turned at 27 and has looked 27 ever since.
- **Wound:** when the Blight reached the west, it didn't eat House Vane; it _agreed_ with them. Her family's hunger grew until they fed on their own village. Scarlet refused, melted down the family silver into two revolvers and hunted her way east after the thing that had whispered to them.
- **Irony she loves:** she carries silver, the one thing that can kill her, on her hips, as a promise never to become her family.
- **Voice:** amused, precise, aristocratic. Teases like a duelist. Calls the Commander "darling" when she's winning and "Commander" when she's scared. Likes boldness and honesty; dislikes being pitied.
- **Arc:** (B1) she's curious about the mortal who isn't afraid of her · (B3) silver and trust · (B5) she tells the Commander about the Keep, and admits the hunger never fully leaves · (B7) a blood-moon night when she's at her weakest and lets herself be looked after · (B9) confession: her heart hasn't beaten in four hundred years, and around the Commander it does.
- **Future:** the map _Blood Moon Castle_ is the Crimson Keep.

### Yuki Frostveil — the Snow Witch

- **Born** on **Mount Shirahane**, youngest of the Snow Queen's daughters. She is 24, which is very young for a yuki-onna.
- **Wound:** yuki-onna freeze whatever they love. Her sisters' stories all end with a frozen lover in the snow, so Yuki decided never to love anything. Then the mountain went silent: the Blight took her sisters' voices, one by one, and she came down because the quiet was worse than people.
- **Secret:** she's terrified of hurting the Commander, and hides it behind aloofness. An Anchor's warmth is the one thing her cold can't take. She hasn't realized that yet.
- **Voice:** quiet, formal, short sentences; blurts things and then dies of embarrassment. Loves gentleness and patience; hates being rushed.
- **Arc:** (B1) cold hands · (B3) hot water · (B5) her sisters' voices, and why she says so little · (B7) she tests her touch on the Commander on purpose, terrified, and nothing freezes · (B9) the first snow that doesn't melt: she chooses to love someone anyway.

### Kaede Emberhorn — the Oni Flame Dancer

- **Born** in **Hinoe**, an oni village in the southern Ember Mountains. She is 29.
- **Role:** the oni of Hinoe danced the **Ember Festival** every year, a fire dance that keeps the Blight out of the south. Humans enjoyed the festival and feared the dancers.
- **Wound:** one year the humans barred the festival out of fear, the fires went out, and the Blight came through. Hinoe didn't survive. Kaede did, by losing her temper so completely that she burned the Blight back alone. She's afraid of that rage (her tier 3 _Oni Awakening_).
- **Voice:** loud, cocky, generous, physical. Laughs first, hurts in private. Loves nerve and honesty; hates pity and being "handled".
- **Arc:** (B1) festival drinks · (B3) horns · (B5) she tells the story of Hinoe, at dawn, sober · (B7) the rage: she asks the Commander to stop her if she ever loses herself · (B9) she dances the Ember Dance, which an oni only dances for the one who "carries her flame".

### Selene Moonwhisper — the Moon Priestess

- **Born** at the Moonlit Shrine and raised by its priestesses. She is 26 and the last of them.
- **Role:** she hears the Lady's song and has been singing the cracking seal shut alone since Keeper Haruo left. She summoned the Commander.
- **Secret:** the old texts say the priestess who sings the **final seal** on the night of the Crimson Eclipse becomes moonlight herself. Selene has always assumed that will be her, and plays mischievous because she decided long ago to enjoy the time she had.
- **Voice:** gentle, playful, a little theatrical ("ara~", "my, my"). Loves romance and sincerity. Deflects with jokes when anything gets close to the truth.
- **Arc:** (B1) a private recital · (B3) fortune telling · (B5) the Moongate: she shows the Commander the cracks and tells them about Haruo · (B7) the Commander finds the old texts; she admits the price · (B9) she chooses to live: "Then we'll find another way. Together. I've decided to be selfish."

## Relationships between the heroines

- **Scarlet ↔ Selene:** old rivals in elegance; secretly each other's confidante.
- **Kaede ↔ Yuki:** Kaede adopted Yuki on day one ("little snowball"), introduced her to hot springs and sake, and is fiercely protective of her.
- **Scarlet ↔ Kaede:** constant bickering about fire and silver; drinking buddies.
- **Everyone ↔ the Commander:** a friendly rivalry for their attention, which the heroines tease each other about.

## Writing rules for chats

1. One scene, one feeling. 8–12 nodes, two choices, each ending on a line she'd actually say.
2. The best choice fits _her_: Scarlet wants boldness, Yuki gentleness, Kaede nerve, Selene romance and sincerity.
3. Every chat from Bond 5 up should reveal one piece of her lore above. Bond 9 is the confession.
4. Use `speaker: 'you'` sparingly for the Commander's spoken lines when the choice text alone isn't enough.
5. Moods must be one of: smile, tease, smirk, wink, laugh, blush, shy, pout, grin.
